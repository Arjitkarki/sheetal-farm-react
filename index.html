Welcome

Sheetal Farm sits on a ridge in the Tansen region of Palpa, a working farm turned resort built for slow mornings and long dinners. The cottages are brick and timber, the garden runs to roses and prayer flags, and a short walk up the hill gets you a bench looking straight down into the valley. We cook the way the farm feeds itself — khaja sets, sukuti, dhido, and whatever came off the fire that day — and keep the rest simple: good tea, good company, and enough quiet to hear the hills.

The Farm

Brick cottages and the ridge

A-frame and brick-and-timber cottages set around a lawn and rose garden, with a hilltop bench looking out over the Tansen valley.

 The Kitchen

Khaja sets, momos, sekuwa

Farm-style Nepali cooking — chukauni, chhoila, sukuti, and khana sets, plus noodles, pizza, and a full bar.

 The Evening

BBQ, music, a morning hike

Overnight packages run from afternoon snacks through a barbecue dinner to a 7am hike before breakfast.
