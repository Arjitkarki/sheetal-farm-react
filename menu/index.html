Farm Packages

Overnight packages, run as a single evening-to-morning schedule.

Sheetal Farm Package

Rs 3500 / person

  • 4:00–9:30 PM · Snacks with music Popcorn, chukauni (Palpali achar), batuk, chhoila, momo, Sheetal aalu, ghore khapang (chicken wings), green salad, a fish item, chicken leg.
  • 8:00–9:00 PM · BBQ
  • 10:00–10:30 PM · Dinner Plain rice, local kukhura or khasiko jhol, saag, gholbhedako achar.
  • Next day, 7 AM Hot lemon with honey, then a one-hour hike.
  • 8:00–9:30 AM · Breakfast Fapar or kodo ko roti, sliced bread, chana masala or potato curry, boiled eggs, muffins, coffee or tea.

Children ages 6–12 are charged half price; ages 12 and up are considered adults. Special arrangements can be made for vegetarians.

Sheetal Farm Mini Package

Rs 3000 / person

  • 6:00–8:00 PM · Snacks with music Chhoila set (chiura, bhatmas, chukauni, batuk), then momo, Sheetal aalu, ghore khapang (chicken wings). Snacks are limited.
  • 8:00–9:00 PM · BBQ
  • 10:00–10:30 PM · Dinner Plain rice, local kukhura or khasiko jhol, saag, gholbhedako achar.
  • Next day, 7 AM Hot lemon with honey, then a one-hour hike.
  • 8:00–9:30 AM · Breakfast Bread with jam, muffins, boiled eggs, kabuli channa or aalu jira.

Children ages 5 and up are charged as adults. Special arrangements can be made for vegetarians. This package is not available on weekends.

Music is turned off after 10:30 PM. Staff services are not available from 10:30 PM to 7:00 AM.
